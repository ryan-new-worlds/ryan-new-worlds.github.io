Golden Dream Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what data the Golden Dream app for Android ("the app", "we") handles. In short: the app works fully offline, has no accounts and no servers of our own, and does not collect or send any data. The app has no ads and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Everything the app creates stays on your device: your character's life, progress, and settings.

  • No account. You do not sign in, and you do not give us your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.
  • No internet connection used. The app does not need, and does not request, network access to play.
  • Android backup. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the app's data to your Google account, as it does for other apps. Google handles this backup under its own privacy policy. You can turn it off in your device settings.
  • Sharing a card image. The app can create a shareable image of your character (a card) and open Android's own share sheet so you can send it wherever you choose. We do not receive this image or know where you share it.

Purchases (Google Play)

The app offers optional one-time support purchases. These are handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment and tells the app which purchase you made, so the app can unlock the matching content. We do not operate any server, and no purchase or payment information is sent to us or to any other third party.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting, and we do not track how you use the app.
  • We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Children

Golden Dream is not directed at children. See the app's rating and target age on its Google Play store listing.

Deleting your data

All of the app's data is only on your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
