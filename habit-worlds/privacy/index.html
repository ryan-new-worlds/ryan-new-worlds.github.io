Habit Worlds Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what data the Habit Worlds app for Android ("the app", "we") handles. In short: your habits and progress stay on your device. We have no accounts and no servers of our own, and the app has no ads and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter in the app stays on your device: your habits, check-ins, reminders, player name, game progress, and settings. We cannot see or access this data.

  • No account. You do not sign in, and you do not give us your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.
  • Android backup. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the app's data to your Google account, as it does for other apps. Google handles this backup under its own privacy policy. You can turn it off in your device settings.
  • Backup files. You can export your data to a file and import it later. The file is plain JSON and is not encrypted. It contains your habit names and history. You choose where to save or share it, so please keep it somewhere safe.
  • Reminders. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Data sent to third parties

The app sends data off your device only in the cases below. None of them include your habit names, history, or player name.

Crash reports (Sentry), only if you turn them on

Crash reporting is off by default. If you turn on "Send crash reports" in Settings, the app sends technical information about errors to Sentry: the error message and stack trace, the app version, basic device information such as the device model and Android version, and a random ID created when the app is installed, which is not linked to your identity. Before sending, the app replaces your habit names, your player name, and any email address in the report with "[redacted]". The app is set up not to send personal information such as your device name, and Sentry is set up not to store IP addresses. The data is stored in the United States. You can turn crash reporting off at any time in Settings.

Purchases (RevenueCat and Google Play)

Payments are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. To know which features you have unlocked, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous ID created for the app, your purchase and subscription information, and basic device information. RevenueCat does not receive your habit data.

App updates (Google Play)

When you open the app, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can offer you the update. Google Play handles this request under Google's privacy policy.

Feedback by email

If you use "Send feedback", your email app opens with the app version, device model, and Android version filled in. We receive these, along with your email address and anything you write, only if you choose to send the email.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or track how you use the app.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Children

Habit Worlds is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Your habit data is only on your device. You can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings. Remember to delete any backup files you exported. To ask us to delete data held by Sentry or RevenueCat, email us at the address below. See Delete your Habit Worlds data for the steps, what we delete, and what is kept.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
