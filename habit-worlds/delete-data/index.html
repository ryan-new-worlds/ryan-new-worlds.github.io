Delete your Habit Worlds data

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This page explains how to delete data from the Habit Worlds app for Android. Habit Worlds has no accounts, and your habits and progress are stored only on your device. A small amount of data is held by the services the app uses for purchases and optional crash reports. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Delete the data on your device

You can do this yourself at any time. We cannot see or delete this data for you.

  • Uninstall Habit Worlds, or open Android Settings → Apps → Habit Worlds → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your habits, check-ins, progress, and settings.
  • Delete any backup files you exported from the app.
  • If Android backup is on, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your Google account. You can manage device backups in your Google account (Google Drive → Backups).

Ask us to delete the data we hold

  1. Email betterlelife@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Habit Worlds data".
  2. If you bought Habit Worlds Pro, include your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA."). You can find it in your Google Play receipt email, or in the Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history. We need it to find your purchase record, because we do not know your name or email address.
  3. We will delete the data listed below and reply to confirm within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Purchase record at RevenueCat: the random anonymous ID created for the app, your purchase and subscription history, and the basic device information stored with them.
  • Feedback emails you sent us from the app.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Crash reports (Sentry): sent only if you turned on "Send crash reports". They do not contain your name, email address, or habit names, so we cannot match them to you. They are deleted automatically 30 days after they are received.
  • Google Play order records: Google keeps its own records of your payments under Google's privacy policy. We cannot delete them.
  • Your Pro purchase: because the purchase stays in Google Play, if you open Habit Worlds again on a device with the same Google account, Pro is restored and a new anonymous purchase record is created at RevenueCat. If you do not want this, uninstall the app before you send your request.
  • Your deletion request: we keep our email conversation about the request for up to 30 days after we confirm it, then delete it.
